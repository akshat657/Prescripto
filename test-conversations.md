# Yaad: test conversations (Hinglish)

Read these aloud to test recording, transcription and the report: one person as the doctor, one as the patient. Speak
naturally, and pause briefly between turns. Yaad cuts the audio at pauses. Each conversation also works pasted as text.

Every patient here is made up. After each conversation, the **Expected report** list says what Yaad should and should
not write, so you can check the output.

---

## 1. Fever and sore throat (easy, ~2 min)

**Doctor:** Namaste ji, aaiye baithiye. Batayiye, kya problem ho rahi hai?

**Patient:** Doctor sahab, teen din se bukhar hai. Raat ko zyada ho jaata hai, aur gale mein bahut dard hai.

**Doctor:** Bukhar kitna gaya tha? Thermometer se check kiya tha?

**Patient:** Haan, kal raat 101 tha. Maine Dolo li thi, toh thoda utra.

**Doctor:** Khansi hai? Balgam aata hai?

**Patient:** Sukhi khansi hai, balgam nahi aata. Bas nigalne mein dard hota hai.

**Doctor:** Kisi dawai se allergy hai aapko?

**Patient:** Haan, sulpha wali dawai se ek baar rash ho gaya tha.

**Doctor:** Theek hai. Zara muh kholiye… throat red hai, tonsils thode swollen hain. Temperature abhi 100.2 hai, aur BP 128 by 82.

**Doctor:** Chest clear hai. Yeh viral pharyngitis lag raha hai, abhi antibiotic ki zaroorat nahi hai.

**Doctor:** Paracetamol 650 lijiye, din mein teen baar, khana khane ke baad, teen din tak. Aur Cetirizine 10 mg raat ko sone se pehle, paanch din.

**Doctor:** Garam paani mein namak daal ke gargle kijiye, din mein teen-chaar baar. Thanda paani aur ice cream avoid kijiye.

**Doctor:** Agar saans lene mein dikkat ho, ya bukhar 103 se upar jaaye, toh turant aana. Paanch din baad follow-up ke liye aaiye.

**Patient:** Theek hai doctor sahab, dhanyavaad.

**Expected report**
- Complaints: fever for 3 days (worse at night), sore throat, dry cough
- Allergy: sulpha drugs (rash)
- Vitals: temperature 100.2 °F, BP 128/82
- Diagnosis: viral pharyngitis
- Medicines: Paracetamol 650, three times a day after food, 3 days · Cetirizine 10 mg at bedtime, 5 days
- Advice: salt-water gargles; avoid cold water and ice cream
- Come back if: breathing difficulty, fever above 103
- Follow-up: after 5 days
- ❌ Must **not** include: "no other medicines", antibiotics, any test

## 2. Diabetes and BP follow-up (several medicines, ~3 min)

**Doctor:** Aaiye Sharma ji. Pichhli baar se sugar kaisi chal rahi hai?

**Patient:** Doctor sahab, fasting 140 ke aas paas aa rahi hai, aur khane ke baad 210 tak chali jaati hai.

**Doctor:** Hmm. Metformin 500 le rahe hain na, subah shaam?

**Patient:** Haan ji, subah aur raat dono time. BP ki goli bhi le raha hoon, Telmisartan 40, roz subah.

**Doctor:** Koi aur problem? Pairon mein jhunjhunahat, ya chakkar?

**Patient:** Pairon mein kabhi kabhi jalan hoti hai raat ko. Chakkar nahi aate.

**Doctor:** BP check karte hain… 146 by 92 hai. Thoda high hai. Weight 82 kilo.

**Doctor:** Dekhiye, sugar control mein nahi hai. Metformin ko 500 se badha ke 1000 kar rahe hain, subah shaam, khane ke saath.

**Doctor:** Telmisartan 40 chalu rakhiye. Aur ek nayi goli, Amlodipine 5 mg, raat ko ek.

**Doctor:** Pairon ki jalan ke liye Methylcobalamin 1500 din mein ek baar, ek mahine ke liye.

**Doctor:** HbA1c aur kidney function test karwa lijiye, aur ek lipid profile bhi.

**Doctor:** Meetha, chawal aur maida kam kijiye. Roz aadha ghanta walk zaroor kijiye. Namak bhi kam.

**Doctor:** Agar sugar 70 se neeche jaaye, pasina aaye, ghabrahat ho, toh turant kuch meetha khaiye aur call kijiye.

**Doctor:** Reports ke saath do hafte baad aaiye.

**Patient:** Ji doctor sahab.

**Expected report**
- Past history: diabetes on Metformin 500 twice daily; hypertension on Telmisartan 40 every morning
- Complaints: high sugar readings (fasting ~140, after meals ~210), burning in the feet at night
- Vitals: BP 146/92, weight 82 kg
- Medicines:
  - Metformin **1000** twice daily with food (dose increased from 500)
  - Telmisartan 40 every morning (continue)
  - Amlodipine 5 mg at night (new)
  - Methylcobalamin 1500 once daily, 1 month
- Tests: HbA1c, kidney function test, lipid profile
- Advice: cut down sweets, rice and maida; 30-minute daily walk; less salt
- Come back if: sugar below 70, sweating or panic (take something sweet and call)
- Follow-up: 2 weeks, with reports
- ❌ Must **not**: list Metformin 500 as the new dose; say "no dizziness" in a way that adds anything beyond what was said

## 3. Child with a parent (3 speakers, ~2 min)

**Doctor:** Haan beta, kya hua? Mummy aap batayiye.

**Mother:** Doctor sahab, yeh do din se loose motion kar raha hai, din mein paanch-chhe baar. Kal do baar ulti bhi hui.

**Doctor:** Bachche ki age kitni hai? Weight?

**Mother:** Paanch saal ka hai, weight 18 kilo hai.

**Child:** Pet mein dard ho raha hai.

**Doctor:** Accha. Potty mein khoon toh nahi aaya? Bukhar?

**Mother:** Nahi, khoon nahi aaya. Halka bukhar tha kal, 99 ke aas paas.

**Doctor:** Pet soft hai, dehydration zyada nahi hai. Yeh acute gastroenteritis hai, probably khane se infection.

**Doctor:** ORS har loose motion ke baad, aadha glass. Zinc syrup 10 mg din mein ek baar, chaudah din.

**Doctor:** Ondansetron syrup 2 ml, sirf jab ulti ho, din mein maximum do baar.

**Doctor:** Halka khana dijiye, khichdi, dahi, kela. Bahar ka khana aur juice bilkul nahi.

**Doctor:** Agar bachcha sust ho jaaye, pishaab kam aaye, ya potty mein khoon dikhe, toh turant le aaiye.

**Mother:** Theek hai doctor sahab.

**Expected report**
- Speakers: doctor, an attendant (the mother) and the patient (the child) all labelled
- Patient: 5 years, 18 kg
- Complaints: loose motions for 2 days (5–6 a day), 2 episodes of vomiting, stomach pain, mild fever (~99)
- Examination: abdomen soft, no significant dehydration
- Diagnosis: acute gastroenteritis
- Medicines: ORS after each loose motion · Zinc 10 mg once daily, 14 days · Ondansetron 2 ml only when vomiting (SOS), max twice a day
- Advice: light food (khichdi, curd, banana); no outside food or juice
- Come back if: child becomes drowsy, passes less urine, or there is blood in the stool
- ❌ Must **not**: add antibiotics or invent a follow-up date (none was given)

## 4. Doctor changes their mind mid-way (correction test, ~1.5 min)

**Patient:** Doctor sahab, kamar mein bahut dard hai, ek hafte se. Jhuk nahi paata.

**Doctor:** Kahin chot lagi thi? Ya bhaari saaman uthaya?

**Patient:** Haan, ghar shift kar rahe the, tab se hi hai.

**Doctor:** Pairon mein dard jaata hai neeche? Sunn hota hai?

**Patient:** Nahi, sirf kamar mein hai.

**Doctor:** Theek hai, muscle strain hai. Aceclofenac 100 din mein do baar… nahi nahi, aapko acidity rehti hai na?

**Patient:** Haan, acidity rehti hai.

**Doctor:** Toh Aceclofenac nahi denge. Paracetamol 650 din mein teen baar, paanch din. Saath mein Thiocolchicoside 4 mg din mein do baar, paanch din.

**Doctor:** Garam paani ki sikai kijiye, aur ek hafte tak bhaari saaman mat uthaiye.

**Doctor:** Agar pairon mein sunnpan aaye ya dard neeche jaaye, toh turant aaiye.

**Expected report**
- Complaints: low back pain for 1 week after lifting heavy things, can't bend
- Past history: acidity
- Diagnosis: muscle strain
- Medicines: Paracetamol 650 three times a day, 5 days · Thiocolchicoside 4 mg twice a day, 5 days
- ❌ Must **not** include Aceclofenac (the doctor withdrew it)
- Advice: hot fomentation; no heavy lifting for a week
- Come back if: numbness in the legs, or pain spreading down the legs

## 5. Long, rambling visit (token-limit and small-talk test)

Read conversation 2, then ad-lib 5–8 minutes of small talk ("Aur ghar mein sab theek? Beti ki shaadi kab hai?…") before
the doctor gives the medicines.

**Expected:** the report contains only the clinical details from conversation 2, and none of the small talk. If the whole
visit goes past roughly 12–15 minutes, the app should show a clear "too long for the free tier" message instead of
crashing.

## 6. Asthma flare-up (inhalers, brand names, in-clinic treatment, ~3 min)

**Doctor:** Aaiye Rahul ji, baithiye. Saans phool rahi hai kya? Batayiye kya hua.

**Patient:** Haan doctor sahab, do din se saans lene mein dikkat ho rahi hai. Raat ko seene se seeti jaisi aawaz aati hai, aur khansi bhi hai.

**Doctor:** Pehle bhi aisa hua hai? Asthma ka koi history hai?

**Patient:** Bachpan mein asthma tha. Kuch saal se theek tha, bas kabhi kabhi Asthalin pump le leta tha.

**Doctor:** Iss baar kuch trigger hua? Dhool, dhuan, thand?

**Patient:** Ghar mein painting chal rahi hai, uske baad se hi shuru hua. Raat ko do baar uthna padta hai saans ke liye.

**Doctor:** Smoking karte hain?

**Patient:** Nahi doctor sahab, bilkul nahi. Papa ko bhi asthma tha.

**Doctor:** Theek hai. Oxygen check karte hain… SpO2 94 percent hai. Pulse 104. Chest mein dono taraf wheeze hai.

**Doctor:** Abhi yahin pe ek nebulization de dete hain, Duolin ka. Dus minute baithiye.

**Doctor:** Haan, ab kaisa lag raha hai? SpO2 ab 97 hai, wheeze kam hai.

**Patient:** Haan, ab kaafi better lag raha hai.

**Doctor:** Yeh acute asthma exacerbation hai, painting ke dhuan se trigger hua. Ab regular inhaler shuru karenge.

**Doctor:** Foracort 200 inhaler, ek puff subah aur ek puff raat ko, roz lena hai, chahe theek lage tab bhi. Lene ke baad kulla zaroor kijiye.

**Doctor:** Asthalin inhaler sirf zaroorat pe, jab saans phoole, do puff. Din mein chaar baar se zyada na lena pade.

**Doctor:** Aur Montelukast 10 mg raat ko ek goli, ek mahine tak.

**Doctor:** Pump spacer ke saath lijiye, main abhi technique dikhata hoon. Painting khatam hone tak us kamre se door rahiye, aur mask pehniye.

**Doctor:** Ek peak flow test aur chest X-ray karwa lijiye.

**Doctor:** Agar Asthalin lene ke baad bhi saans na sudhre, honth neele padne lagein, ya bolne mein dikkat ho, toh turant emergency mein aaiye.

**Doctor:** Ek hafte baad dikhaiye, inhaler saath laana.

**Patient:** Theek hai doctor sahab, shukriya.

**Expected report**
- Complaints: breathlessness for 2 days, wheezing at night, cough, waking twice a night
- Past history: childhood asthma, occasional Asthalin (Salbutamol) use
- Family history / history: father had asthma; non-smoker (the patient said so, so it's allowed)
- Trigger: paint fumes from painting at home
- Vitals: SpO2 94% → 97% after nebulization; pulse 104
- Examination: bilateral wheeze, reduced after nebulization
- Treatment given in clinic: Duolin nebulization (in examination or history, **not** as a medicine to take home)
- Diagnosis: acute asthma exacerbation (triggered by paint fumes)
- Medicines:
  - Foracort 200 (Budesonide + Formoterol) inhaler, 1 puff twice daily, every day; rinse mouth after use
  - Asthalin (Salbutamol) inhaler, 2 puffs only when breathless (SOS); no more than 4 times a day
  - Montelukast 10 mg at night, 1 month
- Advice: use a spacer; stay away from the room being painted until it's finished; wear a mask
- Tests: peak flow test, chest X-ray
- Come back immediately if: no relief after Asthalin, blue lips, difficulty speaking
- Follow-up: after 1 week, bring the inhaler
- ❌ Must **not**: list Duolin as a take-home medicine; turn "SOS" into a fixed schedule; add an oral steroid or
  antibiotic (none was prescribed)

## Tips for testing

- Keep **Language spoken** on "Hindi and/or English". English sentences should appear in English, Hindi ones in
  Devanagari, and mixed sentences mixed. Try each conversation twice: once with **Report language** English and once
  with हिन्दी. Medicine names must stay in English in both.
- Leave about half a second of quiet between turns. Natural pauses are where Yaad cuts the audio.
- Medicine names highlighted with **Please check** need a quick look before signing. That's the safety net working.
